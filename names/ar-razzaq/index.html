ٱلرَّزَّاقُ

Ar-Razzaq

The Provider

Reflection

Ar-Razzaq – The Provider.

Your job, boss, or business is not your ultimate source; they are only channels. Ar-Razzaq is the One who has already written your provision and delivers it through different means. This Name calms the anxiety of chasing dunya: you still work hard, but with trust, not desperation. Ask Ar-Razzaq to bless what you earn and to provide halal sustenance that strengthens your faith.

Direct Qur’an mention

Qur’an 51:58

إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ هُوَ ٱلرَّزَّاقُ ذُو ٱلْقُوَّةِ ٱلْمَتِينُ

Lo! Allah! He it is that giveth livelihood, the Lord of unbreakable might.

English translation: Pickthall.
