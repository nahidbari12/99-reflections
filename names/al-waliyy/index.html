Al-Waliyy

Reflection

Al-Waliyy – The Sole Supporter and Protector.

Al-Waliyy is a close, loving guardian and ally to the believers. This Name comforts you when you feel alone in holding onto your deen or making a difficult choice for Allah’s sake. Remind yourself: I have a Wali who cares for me more than anyone else. Let that closeness motivate you to obey Him and to confide in Him more often.

Direct Qur’an mention

Qur’an 42:9

أَمِ ٱتَّخَذُوا۟ مِن دُونِهِۦٓ أَوْلِيَآءَ ۖ فَٱللَّهُ هُوَ ٱلْوَلِىُّ وَهُوَ يُحْىِ ٱلْمَوْتَىٰ وَهُوَ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَىْءٍ قَدِيرٌ

Or have they chosen protecting friends besides Him? But Allah, He (alone) is the Protecting Friend. He quickeneth the dead, and He is Able to do all things.

English translation: Pickthall.
