Al-Jalil

The Majestic

Reflection

Al-Jalil – The Majestic.

Al-Jalil combines greatness, honor, and might. Reflecting on this Name should fill your heart with respect and awe when you mention Allah. It encourages you to avoid speaking about Him casually or jokingly, and to keep a sense of reverence in your worship. By honoring His majesty, you honor your own soul and elevate your relationship with Him.

Qur’anic context for this attribute

Qur’an 55:27

وَيَبْقَىٰ وَجْهُ رَبِّكَ ذُو ٱلْجَلَـٰلِ وَٱلْإِكْرَامِ

There remaineth but the Countenance of thy Lord of Might and Glory.

English translation: Pickthall.

This traditional Name is connected through the ayah’s wording or meaning rather than appearing as the exact title.
