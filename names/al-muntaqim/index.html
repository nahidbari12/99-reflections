ٱلْمُنْتَقِمُ

Al-Muntaqim

The Avenger

Reflection

Al-Muntaqim – The Avenger.

Al-Muntaqim executes justice against persistent oppressors who do not repent. This Name is not a license for personal vengeance; it is a reminder that ultimate justice is with Allah. It comforts the hearts of the oppressed and restrains you from crossing limits, because you know that wrongdoing will not go unanswered.

Direct Qur’an mention

Qur’an 32:22

وَمَنْ أَظْلَمُ مِمَّن ذُكِّرَ بِـَٔايَـٰتِ رَبِّهِۦ ثُمَّ أَعْرَضَ عَنْهَآ ۚ إِنَّا مِنَ ٱلْمُجْرِمِينَ مُنتَقِمُونَ

And who doth greater wrong than he who is reminded of the revelations of his Lord, then turneth from them. Lo! We shall requite the guilty.

English translation: Pickthall.
