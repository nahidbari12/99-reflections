Reflection

Al-Khabir – The All-Aware.

Al-Khabir knows the hidden details of your situation, your past, and your inner wounds. You do not need to explain every detail when you make du‘a; He already knows what lies underneath your words. This Name should make you comfortable being honest with Allah and careful about the state of your heart, because He is aware of what even you may not fully understand about yourself.

Direct Qur’an mention

Qur’an 6:18

وَهُوَ ٱلْقَاهِرُ فَوْقَ عِبَادِهِۦ ۚ وَهُوَ ٱلْحَكِيمُ ٱلْخَبِيرُ

He is the Omnipotent over His slaves, and He is the Wise, the Knower.

English translation: Pickthall.
