ٱلْغَفَّارُ

Al-Ghaffar

The Constant Forgiver

Reflection

Al-Ghaffar – The Constant Forgiver.

You sin, regret it, and then slip again—but Al-Ghaffar continues to open the door of forgiveness. This Name pushes you away from despair: no matter how many times you return, He is willing to cover, erase, and forgive. Let this encourage you to make sincere tawbah, change bad habits step by step, and never let Shaytan convince you that you are too far gone.

Direct Qur’an mention

Qur’an 38:66

رَبُّ ٱلسَّمَـٰوَٰتِ وَٱلْأَرْضِ وَمَا بَيْنَهُمَا ٱلْعَزِيزُ ٱلْغَفَّـٰرُ

Lord of the heavens and the earth and all that is between them, the Mighty, the Pardoning.

English translation: Pickthall.
