ٱلْمُتَكَبِّر

Al-Mutakabbir

The Supreme

Reflection

Al-Mutakabbir – The Supreme.

True greatness belongs only to Allah. No matter how famous, wealthy, or talented someone becomes, they remain a fragile human in need of sleep, food, and air. Remembering Al-Mutakabbir should remove arrogance from your heart and replace it with humility and gratitude. You are honored not by being “above” others, but by bowing your head to the One who is above all creation.

Direct Qur’an mention

Qur’an 59:23

هُوَ ٱللَّهُ ٱلَّذِى لَآ إِلَـٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ ٱلْمَلِكُ ٱلْقُدُّوسُ ٱلسَّلَـٰمُ ٱلْمُؤْمِنُ ٱلْمُهَيْمِنُ ٱلْعَزِيزُ ٱلْجَبَّارُ ٱلْمُتَكَبِّرُ ۚ سُبْحَـٰنَ ٱللَّهِ عَمَّا يُشْرِكُونَ

He is Allah, than Whom there is no other Allah, the Sovereign Lord, the Holy One, Peace, the Keeper of Faith, the Guardian, the Majestic, the Compeller, the Superb. Glorified be Allah from all that they ascribe as partner (unto Him).

English translation: Pickthall.
