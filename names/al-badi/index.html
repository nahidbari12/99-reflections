Al-Badi

The Incomparable Originator

Reflection

Al-Badi – The Incomparable Originator.

Al-Badi creates in ways no one else can, without copying or needing a model. This Name reminds you that Allah’s solutions and plans for your life can be completely outside your imagination. Do not limit your hopes to what your mind can picture; ask Al-Badi for outcomes that are good in ways you cannot yet see.

Direct Qur’an mention

Qur’an 2:117

بَدِيعُ ٱلسَّمَـٰوَٰتِ وَٱلْأَرْضِ ۖ وَإِذَا قَضَىٰٓ أَمْرًا فَإِنَّمَا يَقُولُ لَهُۥ كُن فَيَكُونُ

The Originator of the heavens and the earth! When He decreeth a thing, He saith unto it only: Be! and it is.

English translation: Pickthall.
