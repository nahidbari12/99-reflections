Ar-Rashid

The Guide

Reflection

Ar-Rashid – The Guide.

Ar-Rashid guides with wisdom, showing you not only the end goal but also the best way to reach it. When you face choices in career, marriage, or major life steps, ask Ar-Rashid to guide you to what is truly best for your deen and dunya. Then move forward with trust, even if the path is not what you originally planned.

Qur’anic context for this attribute

Qur’an 18:10

إِذْ أَوَى ٱلْفِتْيَةُ إِلَى ٱلْكَهْفِ فَقَالُوا۟ رَبَّنَآ ءَاتِنَا مِن لَّدُنكَ رَحْمَةً وَهَيِّئْ لَنَا مِنْ أَمْرِنَا رَشَدًا

When the young men fled for refuge to the Cave and said: Our Lord! Give us mercy from Thy presence, and shape for us right conduct in our plight.

English translation: Pickthall.

This traditional Name is connected through the ayah’s wording or meaning rather than appearing as the exact title.
