As-Sami

The All-Hearing

Reflection

As-Sami – The All-Hearing.

Not a single whisper, tearful du‘a, or quiet complaint escapes As-Sami. Even the thoughts you are too shy to express are known and heard by Him. This Name should encourage you to turn directly to Allah with your secrets, complaints, and dreams. Speak to Him in your own words, at your own pace, knowing that He listens with full attention and mercy.

Direct Qur’an mention

Qur’an 2:127

وَإِذْ يَرْفَعُ إِبْرَٰهِـۧمُ ٱلْقَوَاعِدَ مِنَ ٱلْبَيْتِ وَإِسْمَـٰعِيلُ رَبَّنَا تَقَبَّلْ مِنَّآ ۖ إِنَّكَ أَنتَ ٱلسَّمِيعُ ٱلْعَلِيمُ

And when Abraham and Ishmael were raising the foundations of the House, (Abraham prayed): Our Lord! Accept from us (this duty). Lo! Thou, only Thou, art the Hearer, the Knower.

English translation: Pickthall.
