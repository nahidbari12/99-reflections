ٱلْجَامِعُ

Al-Jami‘

The Gatherer

Reflection

Al-Jami‘ – The Gatherer.

Al-Jami‘ will gather all people on the Day of Judgment and can also gather scattered hearts, families, and opportunities in this life. When things in your life feel scattered and disconnected, ask Al-Jami‘ to gather goodness for you and to gather your heart in His remembrance instead of letting it be pulled in a thousand directions.

Direct Qur’an mention

Qur’an 3:9

رَبَّنَآ إِنَّكَ جَامِعُ ٱلنَّاسِ لِيَوْمٍ لَّا رَيْبَ فِيهِ ۚ إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ لَا يُخْلِفُ ٱلْمِيعَادَ

Our Lord! Lo! it is Thou Who gatherest mankind together to a Day of which there is no doubt. Lo! Allah faileth not to keep the tryst.

English translation: Pickthall.
