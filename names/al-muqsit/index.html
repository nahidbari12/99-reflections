ٱلْمُقْسِطُ

Al-Muqsit

The Just One

Reflection

Al-Muqsit – The Just One.

Al-Muqsit brings fairness, especially where people have been treated unequally. This Name reassures you that Allah is not unaware of imbalances and injustices. In your own life, it encourages you to be balanced, to give each person their due right, and to be fair even when your emotions pull you another way.

Qur’anic context for this attribute

Qur’an 3:18

شَهِدَ ٱللَّهُ أَنَّهُۥ لَآ إِلَـٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ وَٱلْمَلَـٰٓئِكَةُ وَأُو۟لُوا۟ ٱلْعِلْمِ قَآئِمًۢا بِٱلْقِسْطِ ۚ لَآ إِلَـٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ ٱلْعَزِيزُ ٱلْحَكِيمُ

Allah (Himself) is Witness that there is no Allah save Him. And the angels and the men of learning (too are witness). Maintaining His creation in justice, there is no Allah save Him the Almighty, the Wise.

English translation: Pickthall.

This traditional Name is connected through the ayah’s wording or meaning rather than appearing as the exact title.
