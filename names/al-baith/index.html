Al-Ba‘ith

Reflection

Al-Ba‘ith – The Infuser of New Life.

Al-Ba‘ith will resurrect every soul on the Day of Judgment and bring forgotten deeds back to life. This Name is a powerful reminder that this life is not the end. Let it affect your daily choices: forgive when you can, avoid oppression, and invest in good deeds that will rise with you when you meet Him.

Qur’anic context for this attribute

Qur’an 22:7

وَأَنَّ ٱلسَّاعَةَ ءَاتِيَةٌ لَّا رَيْبَ فِيهَا وَأَنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَبْعَثُ مَن فِى ٱلْقُبُورِ

And because the Hour will come, there is no doubt thereof; and because Allah will raise those who are in the graves.

English translation: Pickthall.

This traditional Name is connected through the ayah’s wording or meaning rather than appearing as the exact title.
