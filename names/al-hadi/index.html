Al-Hadi

The Guide

Reflection

Al-Hadi – The Guide.

Al-Hadi guides whom He wills to the straight path and to what is best in each situation. Guidance is the greatest gift you can ask for. Make it a constant du‘a: “O Allah, guide my heart, my choices, and my steps.” Trust that when you sincerely seek guidance and take the means, Al-Hadi will not leave you lost.

Direct Qur’an mention

Qur’an 25:31

وَكَذَٰلِكَ جَعَلْنَا لِكُلِّ نَبِىٍّ عَدُوًّا مِّنَ ٱلْمُجْرِمِينَ ۗ وَكَفَىٰ بِرَبِّكَ هَادِيًا وَنَصِيرًا

Even so have We appointed unto every prophet an opponent from among the guilty; but Allah sufficeth for a Guide and Helper.

English translation: Pickthall.
