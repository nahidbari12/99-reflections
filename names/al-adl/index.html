Reflection

Al-‘Adl – The Utterly Just.

Allah never wrongs a soul, even by an atom’s weight. Every pain, patience, or good deed will either be rewarded or compensated. When life feels unfair, remind yourself that Al-‘Adl will not let your tears or frustrations go to waste. This Name also invites you to practice justice in your family, your work, and your friendships, even when it is difficult or unpopular.

Qur’anic context for this attribute

Qur’an 6:115

وَتَمَّتْ كَلِمَتُ رَبِّكَ صِدْقًا وَعَدْلًا ۚ لَّا مُبَدِّلَ لِكَلِمَـٰتِهِۦ ۚ وَهُوَ ٱلسَّمِيعُ ٱلْعَلِيمُ

Perfected is the Word of thy Lord in truth and justice. There is naught that can change His words. He is the Hearer, the Knower.

English translation: Pickthall.

This traditional Name is connected through the ayah’s wording or meaning rather than appearing as the exact title.
