Reflection

Al-Muqtadir – The Creator of All Power.

Al-Muqtadir’s power is precise and perfectly measured. He arranges events in your life with exact wisdom: who you meet, what you lose, and what you gain. This Name helps you trust the timing and shape of His decrees, even when they differ from what you imagined.

Direct Qur’an mention

Qur’an 54:42

كَذَّبُوا۟ بِـَٔايَـٰتِنَا كُلِّهَا فَأَخَذْنَـٰهُمْ أَخْذَ عَزِيزٍ مُّقْتَدِرٍ

Who denied Our revelations, every one. Therefore We grasped them with the grasp of the Mighty, the Powerful.

English translation: Pickthall.
