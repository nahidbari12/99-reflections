Al-Qahhar

The All-Subduer

Reflection

Al-Qahhar – The All-Subduer.

Al-Qahhar subdues all that tries to compete with Him—false gods, tyrants, and the arrogance of the ego. No matter how strong injustice seems today, it cannot escape His control. When overwhelmed by oppression in the world or struggle in your own soul, remember that surrendering to Allah breaks the hold of everything else. Ask Him to subdue your nafs so it submits to Him instead of controlling you.

Direct Qur’an mention

Qur’an 12:39

يَـٰصَـٰحِبَىِ ٱلسِّجْنِ ءَأَرْبَابٌ مُّتَفَرِّقُونَ خَيْرٌ أَمِ ٱللَّهُ ٱلْوَٰحِدُ ٱلْقَهَّارُ

O my fellow-prisoners! Are divers lords better, or Allah the One, Almighty?

English translation: Pickthall.
