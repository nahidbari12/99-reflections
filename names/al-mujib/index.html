Al-Mujib

The Responsive One

Reflection

Al-Mujib – The Responsive One.

Al-Mujib hears and responds to those who call upon Him, in the best way and at the best time. Sometimes the answer is a clear “yes,” other times it is a delay, a redirection, or protection from something harmful. This Name pushes you to keep making du‘a with hope, even if you have been asking for the same thing for years, trusting that no sincere call is ever wasted.

Direct Qur’an mention

Qur’an 11:61

وَإِلَىٰ ثَمُودَ أَخَاهُمْ صَـٰلِحًا ۚ قَالَ يَـٰقَوْمِ ٱعْبُدُوا۟ ٱللَّهَ مَا لَكُم مِّنْ إِلَـٰهٍ غَيْرُهُۥ ۖ هُوَ أَنشَأَكُم مِّنَ ٱلْأَرْضِ وَٱسْتَعْمَرَكُمْ فِيهَا فَٱسْتَغْفِرُوهُ ثُمَّ تُوبُوٓا۟ إِلَيْهِ ۚ إِنَّ رَبِّى قَرِيبٌ مُّجِيبٌ

And unto (the tribe of) Thamud (We sent) their brother Salih. He said: O my people! Serve Allah, Ye have no other Allah save Him. He brought you forth from the earth and hath made you husband it. So ask forgiveness of Him and turn unto Him repentant. Lo! my Lord is Nigh, Responsive.

English translation: Pickthall.
