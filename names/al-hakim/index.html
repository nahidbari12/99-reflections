Al-Hakim

The All-Wise

Reflection

Al-Hakim – The All-Wise.

Every decree of Allah carries wisdom, even if you cannot see it now. Al-Hakim never acts randomly or unjustly. When something painful happens, remind yourself: there is a wisdom I do not yet understand. This does not remove sadness, but it places it within a frame of trust. Ask Al-Hakim to show you the wisdom behind events and to make you content with His choices.

Direct Qur’an mention

Qur’an 2:32

قَالُوا۟ سُبْحَـٰنَكَ لَا عِلْمَ لَنَآ إِلَّا مَا عَلَّمْتَنَآ ۖ إِنَّكَ أَنتَ ٱلْعَلِيمُ ٱلْحَكِيمُ

They said: Be glorified! We have no knowledge saving that which Thou hast taught us. Lo! Thou, only Thou, art the Knower, the Wise.

English translation: Pickthall.
