ٱلضَّارَ

Ad-Darr

The Distresser

Reflection

Ad-Darr – The Distresser.

Nothing harmful reaches you except by Allah’s permission, and even that harm can carry hidden mercy and lessons. Ad-Darr is not random; He allows certain pains to cleanse sins, raise ranks, and detach you from illusions. This Name helps you accept hardship without blaming Allah, and instead turning to Him for relief and understanding.

Qur’anic context for this attribute

Qur’an 6:17

وَإِن يَمْسَسْكَ ٱللَّهُ بِضُرٍّ فَلَا كَاشِفَ لَهُۥٓ إِلَّا هُوَ ۖ وَإِن يَمْسَسْكَ بِخَيْرٍ فَهُوَ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَىْءٍ قَدِيرٌ

If Allah touch thee with affliction, there is none that can relieve therefrom save Him, and if He touch thee with good fortune (there is none that can impair it); for He is Able to do all things.

English translation: Pickthall.

This traditional Name is connected through the ayah’s wording or meaning rather than appearing as the exact title.
