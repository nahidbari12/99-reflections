Al-Hamid

The Praiseworthy

Reflection

Al-Hamid – The Praiseworthy.

Al-Hamid deserves all praise, regardless of whether we understand His wisdom. We praise Him for what we know and for what we do not know. This Name encourages you to say “Alhamdulillah” in both ease and hardship, recognizing that there is always some mercy, protection, or wisdom hidden in your situation.

Direct Qur’an mention

Qur’an 2:267

يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓا۟ أَنفِقُوا۟ مِن طَيِّبَـٰتِ مَا كَسَبْتُمْ وَمِمَّآ أَخْرَجْنَا لَكُم مِّنَ ٱلْأَرْضِ ۖ وَلَا تَيَمَّمُوا۟ ٱلْخَبِيثَ مِنْهُ تُنفِقُونَ وَلَسْتُم بِـَٔاخِذِيهِ إِلَّآ أَن تُغْمِضُوا۟ فِيهِ ۚ وَٱعْلَمُوٓا۟ أَنَّ ٱللَّهَ غَنِىٌّ حَمِيدٌ

O ye who believe! Spend of the good things which ye have earned, and of that which We bring forth from the earth for you, and seek not the bad (with intent) to spend thereof (in charity) when ye would not take it for yourselves save with disdain; and know that Allah is Absolute, Owner of Praise.

English translation: Pickthall.
