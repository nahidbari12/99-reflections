Al-Wakil

The Trustee

Reflection

Al-Wakil – The Trustee.

Al-Wakil is the One you can rely on to handle your affairs better than you can. When you have done your best—planned, worked, and taken the means—hand the outcome over to Him. This Name eases anxiety about the future: say “Hasbiyallahu wa ni‘mal-wakil” and rest your heart in the knowledge that the One in charge loves you and knows what you do not.

Direct Qur’an mention

Qur’an 6:102

ذَٰلِكُمُ ٱللَّهُ رَبُّكُمْ ۖ لَآ إِلَـٰهَ إِلَّا هُوَ ۖ خَـٰلِقُ كُلِّ شَىْءٍ فَٱعْبُدُوهُ ۚ وَهُوَ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَىْءٍ وَكِيلٌ

Such is Allah, your Lord. There is no Allah save Him, the Creator of all things, so worship Him. And He taketh care of all things.

English translation: Pickthall.
