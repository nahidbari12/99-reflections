Dhul-Jalali Wal-Ikram

Lord of Glory and Honor

Reflection

Dhul-Jalali Wal-Ikram – Lord of Glory and Honor.

This beautiful Name combines Allah’s majesty with His generosity. He is both awe-inspiring and deeply generous to His servants. Remembering it during du‘a—as the Prophet ﷺ taught—helps you speak to Allah with both reverence and hopeful expectation. You stand before a Lord who is majestic, yet welcomes you kindly.

Direct Qur’an mention

Qur’an 55:27

وَيَبْقَىٰ وَجْهُ رَبِّكَ ذُو ٱلْجَلَـٰلِ وَٱلْإِكْرَامِ

There remaineth but the Countenance of thy Lord of Might and Glory.

English translation: Pickthall.
