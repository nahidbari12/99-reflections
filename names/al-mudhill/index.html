NAME 25 OF 99

ٱلْمُذِلُّ

Al-Mudhill

The Dishonourer

Reflection

Al-Mudhill – The Dishonourer.

Al-Mudhill humiliates those who insist on arrogance, mock His signs, or harm others without remorse. This Name is a warning and a protection: do not be among those who misuse their blessings, and trust that Allah will not leave oppression unaddressed forever. Let it keep you far from mocking faith, making fun of others, or feeling “too big” to follow His guidance.

Qur’anic context for this attribute

Qur’an 3:26

قُلِ ٱللَّهُمَّ مَـٰلِكَ ٱلْمُلْكِ تُؤْتِى ٱلْمُلْكَ مَن تَشَآءُ وَتَنزِعُ ٱلْمُلْكَ مِمَّن تَشَآءُ وَتُعِزُّ مَن تَشَآءُ وَتُذِلُّ مَن تَشَآءُ ۖ بِيَدِكَ ٱلْخَيْرُ ۖ إِنَّكَ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَىْءٍ قَدِيرٌ

Say: O Allah! Owner of Sovereignty! Thou givest sovereignty unto whom Thou wilt, and Thou withdrawest sovereignty from whom Thou wilt. Thou exaltest whom Thou wilt, and Thou abasest whom Thou wilt. In Thy hand is the good. Lo! Thou art Able to do all things.

English translation: Pickthall.

This traditional Name is connected through the ayah’s wording or meaning rather than appearing as the exact title.
