Al-Haqq

The Absolute Truth

Reflection

Al-Haqq – The Absolute Truth.

Allah is the ultimate Truth, and everything He informs us of is reality, even if the world denies it. This Name anchors you when trends, opinions, and doubts swirl around you. Align your beliefs, values, and actions with what Al-Haqq has revealed, and you will never be truly lost, even if you feel out of place among people.

Direct Qur’an mention

Qur’an 6:62

ثُمَّ رُدُّوٓا۟ إِلَى ٱللَّهِ مَوْلَىٰهُمُ ٱلْحَقِّ ۚ أَلَا لَهُ ٱلْحُكْمُ وَهُوَ أَسْرَعُ ٱلْحَـٰسِبِينَ

Then are they restored unto Allah, their Lord, the Just. Surely His is the judgment. And He is the most swift of reckoners.

English translation: Pickthall.
