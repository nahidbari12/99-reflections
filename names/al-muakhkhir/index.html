ٱلْمُؤَخِّرُ

Al-Mu’akhkhir

The Delayer

Reflection

Al-Mu’akhkhir – The Delayer.

Al-Mu’akhkhir delays things from you—not to punish you, but often to protect or prepare you. A job, marriage, opportunity, or dua may be delayed because the right conditions are not yet in place. This Name teaches you patience and trust: delay is not denial; it is a different kind of mercy.

Qur’anic context for this attribute

Qur’an 71:4

يَغْفِرْ لَكُم مِّن ذُنُوبِكُمْ وَيُؤَخِّرْكُمْ إِلَىٰٓ أَجَلٍ مُّسَمًّى ۚ إِنَّ أَجَلَ ٱللَّهِ إِذَا جَآءَ لَا يُؤَخَّرُ ۖ لَوْ كُنتُمْ تَعْلَمُونَ

That He may forgive you somewhat of your sins and respite you to an appointed term. Lo! the term of Allah, when it cometh, cannot be delayed, if ye but knew.

English translation: Pickthall.

This traditional Name is connected through the ayah’s wording or meaning rather than appearing as the exact title.
