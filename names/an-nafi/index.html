An-Nafi‘

The Propitious

Reflection

An-Nafi‘ – The Propitious.

Every benefit you receive—guidance, knowledge, health, support—ultimately comes from An-Nafi‘. People are only means. This should make you grateful to Allah first, even as you thank people, and hopeful that He can bring benefit to you from directions you never expected.

Qur’anic context for this attribute

Qur’an 10:107

وَإِن يَمْسَسْكَ ٱللَّهُ بِضُرٍّ فَلَا كَاشِفَ لَهُۥٓ إِلَّا هُوَ ۖ وَإِن يُرِدْكَ بِخَيْرٍ فَلَا رَآدَّ لِفَضْلِهِۦ ۚ يُصِيبُ بِهِۦ مَن يَشَآءُ مِنْ عِبَادِهِۦ ۚ وَهُوَ ٱلْغَفُورُ ٱلرَّحِيمُ

If Allah afflicteth thee with some hurt, there is none who can remove it save Him; and if He desireth good for thee, there is none who can repel His bounty. He striketh with it whom He will of his bondmen. He is the Forgiving, the Merciful.

English translation: Pickthall.

This traditional Name is connected through the ayah’s wording or meaning rather than appearing as the exact title.
