Reflection

Al-Wadud – The Most Loving.

Al-Wadud loves His servants with a pure, constant love that is not based on what they can offer Him—because He needs nothing. This Name heals feelings of worthlessness and loneliness: you are loved by the One who created you. Show your love for Him through obedience, remembrance, and relying on Him in hardship, and know that His love is more loyal than any human love.

Direct Qur’an mention

Qur’an 85:14

وَهُوَ ٱلْغَفُورُ ٱلْوَدُودُ

And He is the Forgiving, the Loving,

English translation: Pickthall.
