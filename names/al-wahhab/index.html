Al-Wahhab

The Supreme Bestower

Reflection

Al-Wahhab – The Supreme Bestower.

Al-Wahhab gives freely, often without you even asking, and without expecting anything in return. Many of your greatest gifts—your parents, your ability to believe, opportunities that came out of nowhere—are pure hibah from Him. When you love this Name, you start seeing life as a series of gifts and you respond by being generous, open-hearted, and willing to give to others for His sake.

Direct Qur’an mention

Qur’an 38:35

قَالَ رَبِّ ٱغْفِرْ لِى وَهَبْ لِى مُلْكًا لَّا يَنۢبَغِى لِأَحَدٍ مِّنۢ بَعْدِىٓ ۖ إِنَّكَ أَنتَ ٱلْوَهَّابُ

He said: My Lord! Forgive me and bestow on me sovereignty such as shall not belong to any after me. Lo! Thou art the Bestower.

English translation: Pickthall.
