At-Tawwab

The Ever-Accepter of Repentance

Reflection

At-Tawwab – The Ever-Accepter of Repentance.

At-Tawwab not only accepts repentance, He repeatedly turns to the servant who turns to Him. No matter how many times you fall, the door is not closed as long as you are alive. This Name transforms guilt into action: instead of sinking into despair, use remorse as energy to rebuild your relationship with Allah.

Direct Qur’an mention

Qur’an 2:37

فَتَلَقَّىٰٓ ءَادَمُ مِن رَّبِّهِۦ كَلِمَـٰتٍ فَتَابَ عَلَيْهِ ۚ إِنَّهُۥ هُوَ ٱلتَّوَّابُ ٱلرَّحِيمُ

Then Adam received from his Lord words (of revelation), and He relented toward him. Lo! He is the relenting, the Merciful.

English translation: Pickthall.
