Surah Ash-Sharh

الشَّرح

The Consolation · Surah 94 · 8 verses

Arabic text: Tanzil Uthmani. English translation: Mohammed Marmaduke Pickthall.

Ayah 1

بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَـٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ أَلَمْ نَشْرَحْ لَكَ صَدْرَكَ

Have We not caused thy bosom to dilate,

Ayah 2

وَوَضَعْنَا عَنكَ وِزْرَكَ

And eased thee of the burden

Ayah 3

ٱلَّذِىٓ أَنقَضَ ظَهْرَكَ

Which weighed down thy back;

Ayah 4

وَرَفَعْنَا لَكَ ذِكْرَكَ

And exalted thy fame?

Ayah 5

فَإِنَّ مَعَ ٱلْعُسْرِ يُسْرًا

But lo! with hardship goeth ease,

Ayah 6

إِنَّ مَعَ ٱلْعُسْرِ يُسْرًا

Lo! with hardship goeth ease;

Ayah 7

فَإِذَا فَرَغْتَ فَٱنصَبْ

So when thou art relieved, still toil

Ayah 8

وَإِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ فَٱرْغَب

And strive to please thy Lord.
