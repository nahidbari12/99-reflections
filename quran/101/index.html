Surah Al-Qaari'a

القَارعَة

The Calamity · Surah 101 · 11 verses

Arabic text: Tanzil Uthmani. English translation: Mohammed Marmaduke Pickthall.

Ayah 1

بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَـٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ ٱلْقَارِعَةُ

The Calamity!

Ayah 2

مَا ٱلْقَارِعَةُ

What is the Calamity?

Ayah 3

وَمَآ أَدْرَىٰكَ مَا ٱلْقَارِعَةُ

Ah, what will convey unto thee what the Calamity is!

Ayah 4

يَوْمَ يَكُونُ ٱلنَّاسُ كَٱلْفَرَاشِ ٱلْمَبْثُوثِ

A day wherein mankind will be as thickly-scattered moths

Ayah 5

وَتَكُونُ ٱلْجِبَالُ كَٱلْعِهْنِ ٱلْمَنفُوشِ

And the mountains will become as carded wool.

Ayah 6

فَأَمَّا مَن ثَقُلَتْ مَوَٰزِينُهُۥ

Then, as for him whose scales are heavy (with good works),

Ayah 7

فَهُوَ فِى عِيشَةٍ رَّاضِيَةٍ

He will live a pleasant life.

Ayah 8

وَأَمَّا مَنْ خَفَّتْ مَوَٰزِينُهُۥ

But as for him whose scales are light,

Ayah 9

فَأُمُّهُۥ هَاوِيَةٌ

A bereft and Hungry One will be his mother,

Ayah 10

وَمَآ أَدْرَىٰكَ مَا هِيَهْ

Ah, what will convey unto thee what she is! -

Ayah 11

نَارٌ حَامِيَةٌۢ

Raging Fire.
