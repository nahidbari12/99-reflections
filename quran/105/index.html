Surah Al-Fil

الفِيل

The Elephant · Surah 105 · 5 verses

Arabic text: Tanzil Uthmani. English translation: Mohammed Marmaduke Pickthall.

Ayah 1

بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَـٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ أَلَمْ تَرَ كَيْفَ فَعَلَ رَبُّكَ بِأَصْحَـٰبِ ٱلْفِيلِ

Hast thou not seen how thy Lord dealt with the owners of the Elephant?

Ayah 2

أَلَمْ يَجْعَلْ كَيْدَهُمْ فِى تَضْلِيلٍ

Did He not bring their stratagem to naught,

Ayah 3

وَأَرْسَلَ عَلَيْهِمْ طَيْرًا أَبَابِيلَ

And send against them swarms of flying creatures,

Ayah 4

تَرْمِيهِم بِحِجَارَةٍ مِّن سِجِّيلٍ

Which pelted them with stones of baked clay,

Ayah 5

فَجَعَلَهُمْ كَعَصْفٍ مَّأْكُولٍۭ

And made them like green crops devoured (by cattle)?
