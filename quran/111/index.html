Surah Al-Masad

المَسَد

The Palm Fibre · Surah 111 · 5 verses

Arabic text: Tanzil Uthmani. English translation: Mohammed Marmaduke Pickthall.

Ayah 1

بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَـٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ تَبَّتْ يَدَآ أَبِى لَهَبٍ وَتَبَّ

The power of Abu Lahab will perish, and he will perish.

Ayah 2

مَآ أَغْنَىٰ عَنْهُ مَالُهُۥ وَمَا كَسَبَ

His wealth and gains will not exempt him.

Ayah 3

سَيَصْلَىٰ نَارًا ذَاتَ لَهَبٍ

He will be plunged in flaming Fire,

Ayah 4

وَٱمْرَأَتُهُۥ حَمَّالَةَ ٱلْحَطَبِ

And his wife, the wood-carrier,

Ayah 5

فِى جِيدِهَا حَبْلٌ مِّن مَّسَدٍۭ

Will have upon her neck a halter of palm-fibre.
