Surah At-Takwir

التَّكوير

The Overthrowing · Surah 81 · 29 verses

Arabic text: Tanzil Uthmani. English translation: Mohammed Marmaduke Pickthall.

Ayah 1

بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَـٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ إِذَا ٱلشَّمْسُ كُوِّرَتْ

When the sun is overthrown,

Ayah 2

وَإِذَا ٱلنُّجُومُ ٱنكَدَرَتْ

And when the stars fall,

Ayah 3

وَإِذَا ٱلْجِبَالُ سُيِّرَتْ

And when the hills are moved,

Ayah 4

وَإِذَا ٱلْعِشَارُ عُطِّلَتْ

And when the camels big with young are abandoned,

Ayah 5

وَإِذَا ٱلْوُحُوشُ حُشِرَتْ

And when the wild beasts are herded together,

Ayah 6

وَإِذَا ٱلْبِحَارُ سُجِّرَتْ

And when the seas rise,

Ayah 7

وَإِذَا ٱلنُّفُوسُ زُوِّجَتْ

And when souls are reunited,

Ayah 8

وَإِذَا ٱلْمَوْءُۥدَةُ سُئِلَتْ

And when the girl-child that was buried alive is asked

Ayah 9

بِأَىِّ ذَنۢبٍ قُتِلَتْ

For what sin she was slain,

Ayah 10

وَإِذَا ٱلصُّحُفُ نُشِرَتْ

And when the pages are laid open,

Ayah 11

وَإِذَا ٱلسَّمَآءُ كُشِطَتْ

And when the sky is torn away,

Ayah 12

وَإِذَا ٱلْجَحِيمُ سُعِّرَتْ

And when hell is lighted,

Ayah 13

وَإِذَا ٱلْجَنَّةُ أُزْلِفَتْ

And when the Garden is brought nigh,

Ayah 14

عَلِمَتْ نَفْسٌ مَّآ أَحْضَرَتْ

(Then) every soul will know what it hath made ready.

Ayah 15

فَلَآ أُقْسِمُ بِٱلْخُنَّسِ

Oh, but I call to witness the planets,

Ayah 16

ٱلْجَوَارِ ٱلْكُنَّسِ

The stars which rise and set,

Ayah 17

وَٱلَّيْلِ إِذَا عَسْعَسَ

And the close of night,

Ayah 18

وَٱلصُّبْحِ إِذَا تَنَفَّسَ

And the breath of morning

Ayah 19

إِنَّهُۥ لَقَوْلُ رَسُولٍ كَرِيمٍ

That this is in truth the word of an honoured messenger,

Ayah 20

ذِى قُوَّةٍ عِندَ ذِى ٱلْعَرْشِ مَكِينٍ

Mighty, established in the presence of the Lord of the Throne,

Ayah 21

مُّطَاعٍ ثَمَّ أَمِينٍ

(One) to be obeyed, and trustworthy;

Ayah 22

وَمَا صَاحِبُكُم بِمَجْنُونٍ

And your comrade is not mad.

Ayah 23

وَلَقَدْ رَءَاهُ بِٱلْأُفُقِ ٱلْمُبِينِ

Surely he beheld Him on the clear horizon.

Ayah 24

وَمَا هُوَ عَلَى ٱلْغَيْبِ بِضَنِينٍ

And he is not avid of the Unseen.

Ayah 25

وَمَا هُوَ بِقَوْلِ شَيْطَـٰنٍ رَّجِيمٍ

Nor is this the utterance of a devil worthy to be stoned.

Ayah 26

فَأَيْنَ تَذْهَبُونَ

Whither then go ye?

Ayah 27

إِنْ هُوَ إِلَّا ذِكْرٌ لِّلْعَـٰلَمِينَ

This is naught else than a reminder unto creation,

Ayah 28

لِمَن شَآءَ مِنكُمْ أَن يَسْتَقِيمَ

Unto whomsoever of you willeth to walk straight.

Ayah 29

وَمَا تَشَآءُونَ إِلَّآ أَن يَشَآءَ ٱللَّهُ رَبُّ ٱلْعَـٰلَمِينَ

And ye will not, unless (it be) that Allah willeth, the Lord of Creation.
