Surah Al-Asr

العَصر

The Declining Day, Epoch · Surah 103 · 3 verses

Arabic text: Tanzil Uthmani. English translation: Mohammed Marmaduke Pickthall.

Ayah 1

بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَـٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ وَٱلْعَصْرِ

By the declining day,

Ayah 2

إِنَّ ٱلْإِنسَـٰنَ لَفِى خُسْرٍ

Lo! man is a state of loss,

Ayah 3

إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوا۟ وَعَمِلُوا۟ ٱلصَّـٰلِحَـٰتِ وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلْحَقِّ وَتَوَاصَوْا۟ بِٱلصَّبْرِ

Save those who believe and do good works, and exhort one another to truth and exhort one another to endurance.
