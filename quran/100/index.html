Surah Al-Aadiyaat

العَاديَات

The Chargers · Surah 100 · 11 verses

Arabic text: Tanzil Uthmani. English translation: Mohammed Marmaduke Pickthall.

Ayah 1

بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَـٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ وَٱلْعَـٰدِيَـٰتِ ضَبْحًا

By the snorting courses,

Ayah 2

فَٱلْمُورِيَـٰتِ قَدْحًا

Striking sparks of fire

Ayah 3

فَٱلْمُغِيرَٰتِ صُبْحًا

And scouring to the raid at dawn,

Ayah 4

فَأَثَرْنَ بِهِۦ نَقْعًا

Then, therewith, with their trail of dust,

Ayah 5

فَوَسَطْنَ بِهِۦ جَمْعًا

Cleaving, as one, the centre (of the foe),

Ayah 6

إِنَّ ٱلْإِنسَـٰنَ لِرَبِّهِۦ لَكَنُودٌ

Lo! man is an ingrate unto his Lord

Ayah 7

وَإِنَّهُۥ عَلَىٰ ذَٰلِكَ لَشَهِيدٌ

And lo! he is a witness unto that;

Ayah 8

وَإِنَّهُۥ لِحُبِّ ٱلْخَيْرِ لَشَدِيدٌ

And lo! in the love of wealth he is violent.

Ayah 9

أَفَلَا يَعْلَمُ إِذَا بُعْثِرَ مَا فِى ٱلْقُبُورِ

Knoweth he not that, when the contents of the graves are poured forth

Ayah 10

وَحُصِّلَ مَا فِى ٱلصُّدُورِ

And the secrets of the breasts are made known,

Ayah 11

إِنَّ رَبَّهُم بِهِمْ يَوْمَئِذٍ لَّخَبِيرٌۢ

On that day will their Lord be perfectly informed concerning them.
